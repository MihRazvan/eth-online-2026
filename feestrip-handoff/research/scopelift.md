# ScopeLift Fixed Fee Swap: final FeeStrip comparison

Reviewed source and selected tests at commit `e29438ffd2805cb0c53f046d1e2a1a6137a20b85` (pinned March 10, 2026). Tests were read, not executed. ScopeLift's [March 11 announcement](https://scopelift.co/blog/liquidity-provider-fixed-fee-swap) presents a proof of concept for selling future Uniswap v4 LP fees. The [pinned README](https://github.com/ScopeLift/fixed-fee-swap/blob/e29438ffd2805cb0c53f046d1e2a1a6137a20b85/README.md) explicitly says unaudited, proof of concept, and not production ready. No deployed adoption was confirmed by this review. These sources establish source-level precedent, not production validation.

## Consequence for novelty

Do not claim FeeStrip introduces LP fee/principal separation, selling future Uniswap v4 LP fees, fungible ERC20 fee rights, static ranges, fee-growth accounting, maturity series, or pre-maturity recombination. ScopeLift already implements these in a close, publicly available design. It is the strongest direct source precedent found in this review. Hookless compatibility alone is also not a defensible distinction: ScopeLift's vault accepts a `PoolKey` and directly manages positions; its market hook is a separate component.

FeeStrip's defensible positioning is the particular product and settlement composition: **strip one native USDC fee leg from an existing canonical PositionManager NFT; freeze that individual position; issue fixed-supply whole-period claims; determine the exact block-N endpoint through authenticated historical state; return the original NFT after fee capture without waiting for proof; and trade the claim directly against USDC through Aqua/SwapVM.** This comparison supports differentiation from this source, not a universal first-ever claim.

## Material overlap and differences

| Dimension | ScopeLift actual source | FeeStrip final shape |
|---|---|---|
| Underlying custody | `LPVault` accepts token0/token1, directly creates/manages PoolManager positions, and issues fungible VST. Its zero-delta fee collection uses vault-owned positions with `salt: bytes32(0)`. | Escrow one already-existing canonical PositionManager ERC721; preserve and return the same NFT. |
| Range/liquidity | Tick ranges and weights are configured at creation. Deposits/withdrawals change the liquidity in those positions. Static ranges already exist here. | Freeze this NFT's liquidity L and range for the individual series. |
| Fee asset | Separate fee indices and transfers for both pool currencies, with address(0) support for native ETH. | Sell only fees actually earned in the native USDC pool leg; other-leg fees remain residual property. |
| Issuance | Deposit VST before maturity to mint equal PT and YT; additional pre-maturity deposits are allowed. | One fixed original quantity Q for one NFT and a specified whole period. |
| Transfer economics | YT checkpoints sender and recipient before transferring balances. Indexed accrued fees are stored by address and remain with the sender; the buyer begins from its checkpoint. | Claim ownership carries the sold whole period's accrued-but-unpaid income. Ordinary ERC20 transfers must transfer that economic exposure. |
| Maturity boundary | Timestamp MATURITY plus collection/checkpoint state; no historical proof or exact block-N fee endpoint. | Exact ending block N and authenticated historical fee-growth proof; later collection does not redefine entitlement. |
| Return of principal | Redeem VST, then vault withdrawal removes liquidity and returns currencies. No original NFT exists in this model. | Return original NFT after capture and recording the accounting evidence, before historical proof finalization. |
| Early exit | Redeem any equal amount of PT+YT before maturity for the corresponding VST; burning YT claims accrued fees. | Recombine residual NFT receipt with all original Q before maturity and before payouts; whole original NFT returned. |
| Trading | PT/VST yield-tick market; Zapper flash-mints/borrows PT to buy/sell YT indirectly for VST. Market code accepts generic token addresses, but the documented system and Zapper use VST. | Direct fee-claim/USDC market through Aqua/SwapVM; no fixed-face-value PT pricing assumption. |

## Maturity lifecycle must be rewritten

The actual path is more specific than “PT becomes independently redeemable at maturity.” While `ytFeeCollectionClosed` is false, `redeem` requires and burns matching YT even after MATURITY. That first post-maturity redeem sets the flag. Later redeems use the PT-only fee path. `collectFeesToYT` reverts once closed. `YieldToken._claim` calls it unconditionally; `burn` invokes `_claim`; and `claimYTFeesAfterMaturity` invokes that burn. Consequently the latter claim path also reaches the closed-collection revert after the first post-maturity redeem. `claimYTFeesAfterMaturity` itself does not set the closing flag.

This is a source-control-flow observation, not an audit, exploit demonstration, or claim of deployed losses. It is enough to reject a wholesale port: FeeStrip needs independent, persistent rights for every outstanding claim after NFT release, backed by a segregated USDC entitlement finalized for block N. The [test that explicitly checks closure and the revert](https://github.com/ScopeLift/fixed-fee-swap/blob/e29438ffd2805cb0c53f046d1e2a1a6137a20b85/test/FixedFeeSwap.unit.t.sol#L717-L741) and the [first/second post-maturity redeem test](https://github.com/ScopeLift/fixed-fee-swap/blob/e29438ffd2805cb0c53f046d1e2a1a6137a20b85/test/FixedFeeSwap.unit.t.sol#L880-L940) reflect this branch structure. The [standalone post-maturity YT claim test](https://github.com/ScopeLift/fixed-fee-swap/blob/e29438ffd2805cb0c53f046d1e2a1a6137a20b85/test/FixedFeeSwap.unit.t.sol#L387-L429) exercises a claim before such closure. These tests do not establish FeeStrip's required lifecycle.

## Reuse decision

The [MIT license](https://github.com/ScopeLift/fixed-fee-swap/blob/e29438ffd2805cb0c53f046d1e2a1a6137a20b85/LICENSE) permits source adaptation with its notices retained. Useful source to inspect or selectively adapt includes metadata/mint-burn authorization scaffolding, events/interfaces, test fixtures, and the fee-growth-inside calculation in [`LPVault._syncFees`](https://github.com/ScopeLift/fixed-fee-swap/blob/e29438ffd2805cb0c53f046d1e2a1a6137a20b85/src/LPVault.sol#L819-L873). Use that calculation as a reference for L × fee-growth delta / Q128, while implementing FeeStrip's own exact endpoints, canonical NFT position identity, modular fee-growth arithmetic, rounding, and reserves. Prefer importing standard primitives from their upstream libraries.

Do not copy `FeeTracking`/`YieldToken` unchanged: their address-retained accrual semantics conflict with claims carrying accrued unpaid income. Do not port `LPVault` as custody: it replaces the required original NFT model. Rewrite FixedFeeSwap settlement and recombination around one NFT, fixed Q, capture/proof/finalization states, and separate outstanding-claim reserves. Do not port the PT/VST convergence curve or PT flash-loan Zapper into a claim/USDC market: the fee claim has an uncertain final USDC payout, not a known 1:1 principal redemption value. NFT escrow, historical proof validation, release-before-proof, and Aqua/SwapVM integration are new FeeStrip work relative to this repository.
