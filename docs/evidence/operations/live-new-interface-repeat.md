At the canonical snapshot **11696574**, hash `0x4a9a41d6a9c1bae2a85ca6116fa89e0e8c28195258455b08886d4414a05c65ea`, **9010Q** had been redeemed and **900996 micros** paid to claims. `1499999 captured = 900996 paid claims + 500000 withdrawn residual + 99003 retained reserve`.

A single final burn of all **990Q** would pay **98999 micros**, leaving **4 micros** of permanently segregated dust. The preceding checkpoint's 3-micro dust scenario assumed one final 1000Q burn; this extra split adds one micro. The reserve remains a live liability, and further splits can increase dust.
