**FeeStrip: sell your Uniswap fees upfront, keep your position.** A funded buyer offer pays the LP for a fixed period of native USDC fee income. The original NFT and fixed range remain intact; buyers hold transferable claims, and the residual beneficiary recovers the NFT independently of delayed fee allocation. ScopeLift's [Fixed Fee Swap](https://github.com/ScopeLift/fixed-fee-swap) is credited prior work; no first-ever fee/principal separation claim is made.
