# FeeStrip product research and implementation priorities

Research completed 2026-09-11 by Codex lead and three specialist lanes. Starting implementation: `22a8996`. The user explicitly prioritized the actual product over hackathon requirements. This report is the synthesis; the linked memos retain source detail, immutable revisions, experiments and limitations.

## Recommendation

Make FeeStrip the clearest way to buy and sell a specific position's unpaid fee income, with reliable recovery throughout its lifecycle. The strongest next investments are recoverable capital, understandable rights, executable quotes and dependable endpoint evidence. These follow from the financial contract, observed implementation gaps and primary-source research. Commercial demand, viable trade sizes and repeatable liquidity remain hypotheses requiring real users and markets.

## What changes our product decisions

**A claim needs a persistent explanation of its rights.** Familiar PT/YT terminology creates a specific trap: Pendle's YT represents future income with holder-level accrual and has no remaining future income at maturity. FeeStrip's token continues to carry its share of unpaid whole-period income through maturity and delayed proof. A purchaser can acquire income earned before purchase. The portfolio, purchase review and settlement screen should use the same receipt: native USDC leg, original NFT, original Q, fraction owned, exact endpoint and current allocation state. This is a FeeStrip implementation recommendation; it is not a proposed change to either instrument. [Pendle's YT mechanics](https://docs.pendle.finance/pendle-v2/ProtocolMechanics/YieldTokenization/YT), [FeeStrip financial contract](../../feestrip-handoff/PRODUCT_CONTRACT.md).

**Frozen range is part of the price.** An LP sells both uncertain fee income and the ability to adjust that NFT during the term. Revert's active-range workflow illustrates a competing need; the LP who expects automatic repositioning may reject FeeStrip. ScopeLift's authors describe their similar static-position concept as a proof of concept and suggest short terms. We should test explicit duration presets and comprehension of lost adjustment rights, without presenting a few days as an empirically optimal duration. [Revert Auto-Range](https://docs.revert.finance/revert/auto-range), [ScopeLift's March 2026 article](https://scopelift.co/blog/liquidity-provider-fixed-fee-swap).

**Pool revenue is an incomplete explanation of a particular claim.** The FLAIR paper separates competition among LPs for fee income from adverse selection: two pools with similar aggregate flow can distribute that income differently when liquidity placement differs. Its ex-post metric is useful motivation for exposing range occupancy and liquidity competition, not a ready-made forecast. The 2022 Uniswap v3 risks/returns study likewise models the importance of strategy and management decisions. Neither paper validates a return estimate for this frozen v4 NFT. Our buyer view should disclose source interval, native denomination, coverage, methodology and observed pool configuration, then allow sensitivity analysis. A high historical fee number alone should not rank a claim as a good purchase. [FLAIR, sections 1.3–2](https://uniswap.org/flair.pdf), [Heimbach, Schertenleib and Wattenhofer, 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.08904).

**Users need to see and recover unaccepted capital.** CoW's order workflow exposes open orders, execution conditions and cancellation races. The transferable lesson is to make an order's lifecycle visible and wait for confirmation. FeeStrip's funded offer is a different mechanism: USDC is already escrowed and cancellation is an onchain refund. Inspection found that the existing contract supported buyer cancellation while the app offered no cancellation control. This research pass implements an owned-offer list and exact refund review, including expired offers and the possibility that seller acceptance wins the transaction race. It does not borrow CoW's offchain or free-cancellation assumptions. [CoW limit-order documentation, updated March 12, 2026](https://cowswap.mintlify.app/cow-swap/tutorials/limit), [FeeStrip cancellation implementation](../../contracts/src/FeeStrip.sol).

**Evidence availability and block-hash availability are separate obligations.** The public Sepolia experiment confirmed EIP-2935 hash access through 8191 blocks, while the tested PublicNode endpoint rejected storage proofs even one block old. Extending a checkpoint window cannot reconstruct missing trie nodes. Geth's current archive documentation also distinguishes historical state from historical trie retention. The near-term design should retain a validated witness promptly, preserve its canonical anchor and surface both states. The initial pass hardened acquisition. The subsequent [retention implementation](../../packages/settlement/README.md) now verifies proofs independently and exercises durable local recovery; separate failure domains and provider qualification remain operational work. [EIP-2935](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-2935), [Geth archive requirements](https://geth.ethereum.org/docs/fundamentals/archive), [exact public experiment and reproduction](settlement-infrastructure.md#read-only-public-observation).

**More agent infrastructure is not the current bottleneck.** The engineering review found useful narrowly scoped guidance in Trail of Bits, Uniswap, StreamingFast, Graph, Vercel and Impeccable. It also found executable hooks, mutable secondary references, mismatched package metadata and qualified licensing. Keep native agents, separate worktrees, one integration owner and explicit ownership of ports/nodes/generated files. Apply selected procedures to observed failure classes; installing an entire framework would add maintenance without supplying RPC history, actual wallet evidence or user understanding. Exact source pins and adopt/defer decisions are in the [engineering memo](engineering-workflow.md).

## Ordered backlog and evidence required

These priorities are engineering judgments, not a promised schedule. Existing functionality should be extended against the stated evidence; the table does not imply every feature is absent today.

Public provider qualification requires project access; user-file import remains a follow-up. |
| Locally verified | Reuse one entitlement receipt across buying and holdings | Original-Q amount, prior accrued income travels with tokens, exact N, no token expiry at maturity, and NFT return separate from cash allocation; unaided buyer comprehension test. |
| Locally verified | Improve executable quote and maker controls | Amount-specific bid/ask capacity; stale, revoked, depleted and cancelled states; explicit no-exit-bid result; never count overlapping Aqua capital twice. |
| Next | Measure settlement economics | Full receipt gas and calldata for existing verifier/cache across identical and distinct tuples; publish chain/block/units. Evaluate minimum useful notional from actual costs and quotes, not an assumed ETH price. |
| After access | Real wallet and live data recovery | Injected-wallet rejection/replacement/reload/network changes; live Substreams restart/undo and Subgraph join with source identity; controlled public sale, trade, delayed capture, NFT return, proof and independent redemption. User is gathering access; these gates stay visible. |
| Explore separately | EIP-2935 anchor and proof compression | Chain-specific code/fork checks, boundary/absence/warm-up tests, independent contract review; benchmark savings before library or trust-path changes. |

Defer automated rebalancing, pooled backing, broad chain expansion, a new AMM, mandatory agent frameworks and a coprocessor migration. Each would broaden the architecture or alter the financial proposition before current recovery, economics and usability are established. Do not infer a new approval requirement for routine work; material changes to custody, denomination, exact cutoff or settlement trust remain governed by the product contract.

## What the first user sessions should resolve

Prepare separate seller, buyer and maker tasks. Ask the seller to identify what they lose control over and what assets come back. Ask the buyer to explain fees earned before purchase, the native USDC leg, original Q, maturity and unavailable resale. Ask the maker to publish a bounded quote, reduce available capital and explain the resulting executable size. Observe completion and misunderstandings before teaching the concepts.

Then exercise recovery: reject a signature, refresh during a transaction, cancel an expired offer, return the NFT while proof is pending, and redeem from another wallet. Record the action attempted, observed result, exact app/chain version and any assistance needed. The local unlocked-wallet suite verifies contract interactions; it does not establish production signing behavior or product comprehension. The user's planned end-to-end test supplies evidence that web research cannot.

No new credentials or product decision is needed to continue the local backlog. Remaining access requirements are already recorded in [STATUS](../STATUS.md). Hackathon materials are retained as historical documentation, with no active submission work in this roadmap.
