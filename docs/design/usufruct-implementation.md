# usufruct: orchard, accession form and cabinet

The user’s two supplied visual references are the authority for this change. Their paper background, italic specimen headings, typewriter text, thin rules and fruit/ticket vocabulary now shape the actual application. Reference financial copy and numerical examples were not adopted.

## Implemented workflow

- **Orchard (`#market`)** is a contiguous catalogue of actual adapter markets. Each row shows one fixed period, exact endpoint block, original Q, current executable availability, and USDC price when an executable quote exists. The connected cabinet preview uses actual wallet claim quantities. Disconnected visitors are asked to connect; illustrative ownership is not substituted.
- **Pin a tree (`#pin`)** selects a canonical eligible NFT, shows its frozen position and funded terms, then exposes the existing NFT approval, exact-offer acceptance and buyer funding actions. Acceptance opens My cabinet. The three numbered stages remain the real funded-offer workflow; they do not create arbitrary monthly products or promise unilateral withdrawal.
- **My cabinet (`#positions`)** puts held fee claims first, followed by recoverable offer funding, maker strategies and escrowed NFT lifecycle actions. Receipts, proof recovery, quoting, cancellation and independent NFT return remain available. An unavailable bid occupies one explanatory line instead of a disabled resale form.
- **Claim detail** carries the new typography and specimen language through the existing exact-rights receipt, transaction review, analysis, proof recovery and lifecycle controls.

## Economic and visual boundaries

A tree denotes the original position NFT; fruit denotes fee claims for one agreed period. Each claim carries its share of all unpaid native-USDC income for that whole period, including income accrued before purchase. Original Q remains the denominator. The separate residual right, capture, NFT return, allocation and redemption remain distinct. Early closure still requires all Q plus the residual right; buying claims back is not guaranteed.

Ten catalogue marks divide original Q visually. Availability is computed with exact bigint comparisons, including a partial first mark for quantities below one basis point. The marks do not represent monthly strips, paid income or guaranteed liquidity. Exact numeric quantities remain alongside them. Empty marks mean unavailable inventory, not proven sales. The brand palette is apple `#bd4432`, pear `#afbf61`, quince `#edcc58`, damson `#754466`; fruit color identifies a position rather than its revenue.

No decorative barcode implies a verifiable instrument. NFT number and exact receipt identity provide the accession record. A wallet with zero remaining claims is described as having no unredeemed claims, without claiming that it redeemed them.
